常用接口

GET  /health
GET  /api/<kind>                 kind: consignments | facilities | waybills | stays | lockdowns | pending
POST /api/<kind>
GET  /api/entities/<id>
POST /api/entities/<id>/actions  结论先到为准：expected_version 冲突返回 409
POST /api/offline/sync           断网点登记回连，按现场时间合并，对不上的进待核对
